Entropy Forge Privacy Policy

Effective: November 1, 2026

Entropy Forge ("the app") is a password and key generator for iOS and Android, published by German Carrillo ("we").

Information we collect

None. Entropy Forge does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information or usage data. The app contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking software, and it has no accounts.

Network access

Entropy Forge does not connect to the internet. All generation happens on your device.

Data stored on your device

The app saves your generator preferences (for example password length and selected character types) locally on your device so they are remembered next time. Generated passwords are never saved by the app. These preferences are removed when you delete the app.

Clipboard

When you tap Copy, the generated password is placed on your device's clipboard. On iOS it is restricted to the device (it does not sync to your other devices) and expires after the time you choose. On Android it is marked as sensitive and cleared after the time you choose. Entropy Forge never reads your clipboard.

Children

Entropy Forge does not collect information from anyone, including children under 13.
